# Decision on its claim

Candidate Pattern · PL ME WC VR · Observed. An offered arrangement, not a Registry admission or
content rule (107/117). Choose and change it for the task; no prescribed word count or page schema.

## When it helps
Keep a consequential question, its decisive evidence and every option together in the reading path.

## Components and hierarchy
1. [source-reference](../components/source-reference/README.md) and [evidence-disclosure](../components/evidence-disclosure/README.md) establish the material version.
2. [criteria-list](../components/criteria-list/README.md) is useful for a stage gate; [claim-card](../components/claim-card/README.md) or [truth-change](../components/truth-change/README.md) for a changed item.
3. [decision-context](../components/decision-context/README.md) anchors the Kernel Decision, with recommendation and consequence inside each visible option.
4. [stage-nav](../components/stage-nav/README.md) can group open Decisions; Kernel count/jump and closed-parent badges handle hidden questions.

## Kernel / Keys
Decision key names the judged item; material names evidence. Agent uses atelier ask. Kernel owns optional response note, opened/kept/changed receipt and 10-second undo; one Page owns a cross-Page Decision, other Pages link it.

## Evidence for and against
[VV P4/D-T1](../SOURCES.md#owner), [html-plan](../SOURCES.md#html-plan) decision-on-row, [VR §2 T4](../SOURCES.md#use-cases). Owner wanted optional detail and video-local questions; two closed popups failed.

## Boundaries
No candidate-owned radios, receipt store or clipboard transport. Card-less Decisions remain Kernel-owned in the reading path (capped-height renderer), not silently discarded. Actual app links/video plays are explicit open signals, not evidence of comprehension.
