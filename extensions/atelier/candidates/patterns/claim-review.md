# Live app claim review

Candidate Pattern · WC · Observed. An offered arrangement, not a Registry admission or
content rule (107/117). Choose and change it for the task; no prescribed word count or page schema.

## When it helps
Judge a specific Before/Now change in an exercisable app scenario, and turn new marks into work.

## Components and hierarchy
1. [variant-picker](../components/variant-picker/README.md) and [viewport-frame](../components/viewport-frame/README.md) open the actual build; [observation-stamp](../components/observation-stamp/README.md) names it.
2. [scenario-readiness](../components/scenario-readiness/README.md) distinguishes located / scenario available / observed check.
3. [claim-card](../components/claim-card/README.md) presents verification steps next to the app. [review-bridge](../components/review-bridge/README.md) focuses pins and picks new regions.
4. [review-filter](../components/review-filter/README.md), [progress-groups](../components/progress-groups/README.md) and [item-pager](../components/item-pager/README.md) support review across scopes.
5. [revision-summary](../components/revision-summary/README.md) shows rework without erasing the old Redo. [decision-context](../components/decision-context/README.md) asks adoption choices.

## Kernel / Keys
run-ID/app/claim-ID + actual build version. Bridge locator is evidence beneath that Key. Confirm/Redo is Verdict Record; rework is an explicit Request. Agent done is not human accepted.

## Evidence for and against
[WC §2 T2/T4](../SOURCES.md#use-cases), [World-console](../SOURCES.md#world-console) and [LAU/FL](../SOURCES.md#owner). Real source use: Confirm/Redo and marks. Failure: six Apps confirmed against a 404; no fixture in the human iframe.

## Boundaries
Only confirmed entries may collapse, and they remain reopenable; pending/Redo stay open (FL, local choice). Saved-state auto-advance is adapter work. New marks become stable claim cards through a Request, not a side Comment queue.
