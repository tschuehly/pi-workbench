Your plan: Esslingen Christmas market

One Saturday from lunchtime to evening, home by bedtime. The market usually opens the week before the first Advent Sunday (29 Nov), so check the dates on the city's website first.

  1. 12:00
    S-Bahn S1 from Stuttgart Hbf

    It runs every 15 minutes and takes about 15 minutes. The Deutschlandticket covers it.

  2. 12:30
    Lunch at Mattis, Hafenmarkt 12

    Maultaschen in many kinds, about €15–17 a person. The walk starts right outside. Check Saturday opening times or book: +49 711 74057554.

  3. 14:00
    Old town in daylight

    Walk to the Hafenmarkt, one of the oldest rows of half-timbered houses in Germany, then past St. Dionys church to the Old Town Hall.

  4. 15:45
    Up to the castle walls for sunset

    Climb the covered stairway to the old castle walls. Sunset is around 16:30, with a view over the roofs and vineyards.

  5. 17:00
    Christmas market after dark

    Start on the Rathausplatz, then go on to the medieval market with its costumed traders and craft stalls. Mead or mulled wine to warm up.

  6. 18:30
    Dinner from the stalls

    Flatbread from the wood oven, grilled food and something sweet. Stay for an evening fire show.

  7. 21:00
    S-Bahn home

    Trains run late, so there's no need to rush.

  • Bring cash; some stalls don't take cards.
  • Saturday evenings get crowded. A weekday evening is quieter.
  • Want more? On Sunday, a cellar tour at Kessler, Germany's oldest sparkling wine house, right in the old town. Book ahead.
