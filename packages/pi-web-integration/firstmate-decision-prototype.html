FirstMate decision policy

Logic prototype — press buttons, watch the mate decide.

The question. You are "vibing" across several interactive Pi sessions with loose specs. Each session periodically hits a decision point and pauses. A watching mate either lets the session continue (when it judges the decision safe with reasonable certainty) or notifies you and blocks the session until you answer.

This demo pushes that policy through cases that are hard to reason about on paper: where should the confidence threshold sit? What does "reasonable certainty" cost you? The counter that matters most is decisions the mate let proceed without you — the price of not babysitting.

Not modelled: actually reading a live session or sending it an answer — neither Pi Workbench seam exposes that yet. This prototype only answers whether the policy feels right.
